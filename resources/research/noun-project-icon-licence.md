# Noun Project icon licence — what a free download obliges

Looked up on 2026-08-30 while processing [bot-icon], because the cat drawing in
`assets/` is a Noun Project download and its baked-in credit text gets cropped
away when it becomes an avatar.

The Noun Project offers each icon two ways.

**A free download is Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY 3.0).** You may use,
copy, modify and build on the icon, including commercially, as long as you
credit the creator. Their stated credit format is the icon's name, the
creator's name, and "from Noun Project", with the Noun Project part linked to
the icon's own page. Modifying the artwork does not remove the obligation, so
cropping the credit text out of the file does not settle it — the credit has to
appear somewhere in the work.

**A paid download is royalty-free and requires no attribution at all.** It comes
with a subscription, pre-paid credits, or a single purchase priced at $4.99 at
the time of this lookup.

So the choice for this project is between paying once to drop the obligation, or
keeping the free licence and deciding where the credit line lives — a Discord
avatar has no surface to show one, so it would have to sit somewhere else in the
project.

Not established by this lookup: which of the two licences this particular file
was downloaded under. The file carries the baked-in credit text that the free
download produces, which points at CC BY, but that was not confirmed against a
purchase record.

Sources: thenounproject.com's legal page and its help-centre articles on
licensing and on crediting creators.
