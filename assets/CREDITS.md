# Credits

## The cat drawing

The bot's avatar is built from a cat drawing downloaded from the Noun Project.
The source file is `cat-noun-project-source.svg` in this folder; the avatar made
from it is `bot-icon.svg` and its export `bot-icon.png`.

A free Noun Project download is licensed **CC BY 3.0**. That licence allows use,
modification and commercial use, and it obliges a credit to the creator. Modifying
the artwork does not remove the obligation, so cropping the file's baked-in credit
text out of the avatar does not settle it — the credit has to appear somewhere in
the work. The full finding, including the paid alternative that was declined, is
in `resources/research/noun-project-icon-licence.md`.

The credit line therefore lives on the sign page, which is the surface other
people see and which already credits the photographers. Its wording is:

> Cat drawing by inmyheart from Noun Project

with "inmyheart" linked to the drawing's own page,
https://thenounproject.com/icon/cat-8273703/

## The photographs

The sign page's background photos come from Pexels and each one credits its
photographer in the bar at the bottom of the page, changing with the photo.
